# About Me
I am an Institute Postdoctoral Fellow at the Institute for Computational and Experimental Research in Mathematics in connection with the Spring 2027 program [_Metric Algebraic Geometry: Going Global_](https://icerm.brown.edu/program/semester_program/sp-s27).
My research interests are in applied algebraic geometry, specifically algebraic statistics and metric algebraic geometry.


I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics from UC Berkeley in May 2026, under the 
supervision of [Bernd Sturmfels](https://math.berkeley.edu/~bernd/){:target="_blank"} and [Serkan Hoşten](https://sites.google.com/view/serkanhostensfsu/home?authuser=0){:target="_blank"}.
In my dissertation, [_Metric Algebraic Geometry of Grassmannians_](metric_algebraic_geometry_of_grassmannians.pdf){:target="_blank"}, I study optimization problems on Grassmannians arising in applications from the perspective of algebraic geometry.
I received my B.S. from Harvey Mudd College with High Distinction and Honors in Mathematics.
My undergraduate thesis was advised by [Michael Orrison](https://www.hmc.edu/mathematics/people/faculty/orrison/){:target="_blank"} and is titled [_Long Increasing Subsequences_](https://scholarship.claremont.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=1274&context=hmc_theses){:target="_blank"}.
